How to read your tire size

What does 205/55 R16 91V mean?

Every tire carries its size on the sidewall. Five numbers tell you everything you need.

205
WidthTire width in millimetres
55
ProfileSidewall height as % of width
R
ConstructionRadial — nearly all modern tires
16
RimWheel diameter in inches
91V
Load & speed91 = 615 kg per tire · V = up to 240 km/h
205/55 R16 91V
Tubeless · Radial · DOT 2325
Which type do I need?

Pick the right type for your vehicle

Passenger

Passenger

Sedans and hatchbacks: Corolla, Elantra, Cerato, Accent. Quiet, comfortable, fuel-efficient.

e.g. 185/65 R15 · 205/55 R16 · 225/45 R17

SUV

SUV & crossover

Tucson, Sportage, RAV4, X-Trail, GLC. Mostly tarmac, higher load.

e.g. 225/65 R17 · 235/55 R19 · 255/45 R20

4x4

4x4 AT & MT

Land Cruiser, Patrol, Wrangler, Hilux. All-terrain for road + sand, mud-terrain for rock and mud.

e.g. 265/65 R17 · 285/70 R17 · 285/75 R16

Van

Van & light commercial

Caddy, H1, Sprinter, minibuses. Reinforced sidewall, C load rating.

e.g. 195/75 R16C · 215/70 R15C · 235/65 R16C

Truck & Bus

Truck & Bus

Steer, drive and trailer positions. Retreads and on-site fitting for fleets.

e.g. 295/80 R22.5 · 315/80 R22.5 · 245/70 R17.5

OTR

OTR — off-the-road

Loaders, graders, cranes, mining and construction plant.

e.g. 20.5 R25 · 23.5 R25 · 445/65 R22.5

EV

EV

Tesla, BYD, Ioniq, EV6. More weight, instant torque, silence required — purpose-built compounds.

e.g. 235/45 R18 · 255/45 R19 · 255/40 R20
