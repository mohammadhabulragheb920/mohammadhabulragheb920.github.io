Our story · since 1963

Three generations of knowing tires.

Abu Al-Ragheb Tires is a multi-brand tire, wheel and battery specialist founded by Sharif Abu Al-Ragheb in Mahatta, Amman, Jordan in 1963. Run today by the third generation, it has five branches in Amman, the Faz3a 24/7 roadside service, fleet and government supply — and an operation in the UAE.

Timeline

From one shop in Mahatta

  1. 1963 First shop Sharif Abu Al-Ragheb opens the first tire shop in Mahatta, Amman.
  2. 1967 Fitting & government supply Installation service begins, along with supply to the Armed Forces and government.
  3. 1979 First agencies First official agencies and the start of wholesale.
  4. 1981 Wadi Saqra Wadi Saqra branch opens.
  5. 1993 Al-Madina Al-Madina branch opens.
  6. 2000 Abu Al-Ragheb Tires founded Abu Al-Ragheb Tires is established as the group's tire company.
  7. 2005 Company established Haitham Abu Al-Ragheb Sons Co. is incorporated.
  8. 2010 Pirelli Center Pirelli Center opens at 7th Circle.
  9. 2011 Bayader Bayader branch opens.
  10. 2015 EV tires First dedicated range of tires for electric vehicles.
  11. 2017–2025 New agencies Fuel, KMC, XD, Maxxis Razr, Toyo R888R, Wideway, Turbo, Zeknova and Vitour.
  12. 2024 UAE B2B supply begins in Dubai.
  13. 2026 Faz3a + Gardens + SOFEX Faz3a 24/7 launches, the Gardens branch opens inside Engen station, and SOFEX.
