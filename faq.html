Choosing a tire

Your tire size is printed on the sidewall of your current tire (for example 225/45 R17) and on the sticker inside the driver's door. Send a photo of either to Abu Al-Ragheb Tires on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the size and the brands in stock.

There is no single best brand — the right tire depends on your car, how you drive and your budget. Abu Al-Ragheb Tires has carried every major brand since 1963, from Pirelli, Michelin, Continental, Goodyear and Bridgestone to value brands, so we recommend from the whole market, not from one agency.

Yes, when you buy the right ones. Abu Al-Ragheb Tires has worked directly with Chinese manufacturers since 2015 and only stocks lines that meet the same DOT/ECE standards as European brands. We'll tell you plainly which ones we'd put on our own cars.

Jordan's summers are hot and dry and its winters are short but can bring ice in Amman and the north. For most drivers an all-season tire is the practical choice; sports cars benefit from summer tires; 4x4s that leave the asphalt need all-terrain or mud-terrain tires. Drivers in the highlands should ask us about winter grip between December and February.

Replace tires when the tread reaches 1.6 mm, when you see cracks or bulges, or when they are more than five to six years old — whichever comes first. The DOT date code is on the sidewall; every Abu Al-Ragheb Tires branch checks it for free.
